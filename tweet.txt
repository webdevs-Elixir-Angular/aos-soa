🚀 Benchmarked Array of Structures vs Structure of Arrays in Rust

Result: SoA is 5.3x faster (15µs vs 80µs for 100K elements)

Why? Cache locality + auto-vectorization = 6.5M elements/sec

Manual SIMD on AoS? Actually SLOWER than scalar due to strided memory access.

Data layout matters. A lot.
